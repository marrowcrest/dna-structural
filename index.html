Process

How an Engagement Runs

Five steps from your first message to a sealed set of drawings — and beyond, through permit and construction.

01

Free Consultation & Scope

Share your architectural plans and site details. The initial review costs nothing. You receive a fixed-fee proposal with clearly defined deliverables — no open-ended billing, no surprises later.

02

Structural Analysis & Modelling

Your structure is modelled and analysed against every governing load case — dead, live, wind, and seismic per NSCP. This is the step that determines whether the building stands, and it happens before a single drawing is issued.

03

Design Development & Value Engineering

Members are sized, foundations designed, and everything coordinated with your architect and MEP consultants.

Fees are scoped against the value of the project, not quoted blind. An optimised structure puts steel and concrete only where they earn their place — and on most projects that saving offsets a meaningful part of the engineering fee. Under-designed structures get paid for later; over-designed ones get paid for in material nobody ever sees.

04

Signed & Sealed Documents

Structural plans, details, schedules, and general notes — signed and sealed by a PRC-registered civil engineer, ready for building permit submission.

05

Permit & Construction Support

Plan-check comments answered, contractor RFIs resolved, and site visits as the structure goes up. The engagement does not end when the drawings are issued.
